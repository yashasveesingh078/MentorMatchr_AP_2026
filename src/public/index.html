System Workflow

How It Works.

MentorMatchr replaces chaotic manual spreadsheet matching with an airtight relational database architecture.

Step 01

Controlled Taxonomy

Faculty domains, expertise areas, and technical keywords are stored in normalized master tables to eliminate vocabulary drift and typos.

Step 02

Problem Scoping

Each hackathon problem statement is tagged with a primary domain and required technical keywords, isolated to the event scope.

Step 03

Relational Scoring

A deterministic SQL matching engine weights primary/secondary domain affinity (60%) and keyword tag intersection (40%) into explainable scores.

Step 04

Capacity & Auditing

Partial unique indexes enforce at most one active mentor per team, while application guards ensure no faculty exceeds their maximum team workload.
